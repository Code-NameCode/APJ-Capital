Investment Banking

Capital structure, M&A and private capital access for mid-market companies, designed around your objectives, not around a standard playbook.

At APJ Capital, we begin by getting to know our clients: how their organisation works, where its strengths lie and where it is vulnerable. Only then do we recommend the capital and deal structure best suited to meet their immediate needs while supporting the company’s long-term vision.

We treat every opportunity as unique. Some clients need capital, or a more efficient capital structure. Some need to de-leverage their balance sheets; others need to take on more leverage. Some need access to the private capital markets to fund growth and achieve liquidity, while others are better served by being taken private. Some need strategic direction. Some need best-practice implementation. And some need to tell their story more compellingly to investors and the private capital markets.

Whatever the need, we bring the expertise and reach to structure the right solution.

APJ's international network of funding relationships in the private capital markets, and its procurement network, positions the firm to tailor a value-added process when introducing international clients to the appropriate strategic investors or products. Our team of investment and operating professionals holds local and international market knowledge, allowing us to fully address the corporate financial needs of clients around the world.

What a mandate looks like

  • Bespoke capital structure and deal-term design around the client's commercial reality.
  • Equity and debt raising, matched to the specific deal rather than to a house product.
  • Strategic investments where the firm is a principal, not only an arranger.
  • Private capital market access: growth and liquidity, or taking a company private.
  • Match-making across angels, private equity, venture capital and family offices.
