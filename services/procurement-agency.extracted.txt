      <p>With a diversified network of capital providers and strategic partners, APJ Capital Group is more than a source of funding and investment. We add value across every aspect of our clients’ businesses.</p><h3>Middle East Procurement and Representation</h3><p>The Middle East faces significant demand for infrastructure rebuilding and for commodities, particularly petroleum derivatives. In response, APJ Capital has grown into a procurement and agency-representation firm in the region. Procurement has become integral to corporate performance there, and we have built strong customer-supplier relationships that help our clients achieve sustained cost savings.</p><p>Our tactical services include engagement with senior policy makers across the Middle East and the tracking of government contracts and procurement developments. We assist clients in raising debt and equity capital and, where appropriate, make strategic investments in their businesses alongside them.</p><h3>Our Commitment</h3><p>We give every transaction our full attention, with the aim of generating positive risk-adjusted returns across market cycles. Each opportunity is assessed through rigorous fundamental analysis of the drivers of risk and return, together with a cost-benefit review of both qualitative and quantitative factors, before we proceed to extensive due diligence.</p>
