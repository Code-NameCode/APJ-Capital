Southern Africa Platform

Johannesburg-based market entry, joint ventures and co-investment structures purpose-built for South Africa, Namibia, Botswana and the wider corridor.

  • Investment Banking
  • Procurement & Agency
  • Southern Africa Platform
  • Operational Corporate Execution
  • Market Inflection Point Strategy

In the Southern African channel, we focus on three critical needs: alternative energy, sewage treatment and water purification. Across Sub-Saharan Africa, ageing infrastructure, unreliable power and growing urban demand have made these among the region’s most urgent challenges.

The firm acts as organiser, arranger and advisor for international enterprises looking to establish or expand their presence and businesses in the region. APJ sources and structures cross-border transactions, manages regulatory and commercial complexities, and helps investors access deal flow with strong upside alpha and adjustable risk-return profiles.

APJ Capital draws on its network of technology providers, engineering partners and capital sources in the United States to help design and deliver practical, fundable solutions. We bring together the right technology, the right partners and the right capital structure, positioning our clients and their projects to propose credible solutions for the region.

The platform provides clients with accelerated market entry, localised operational knowledge, regulatory issue management and access to an extensive digitally connected client network. APJ values collective thinking, strong ethics and an innovative mindset to unlock unique opportunities for its stakeholders.
