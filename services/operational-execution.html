Operational Corporate Execution

Negotiation, counterparty relationships and financial execution: the part of a transaction that determines whether it actually closes well.

APJ Capital's goal is to offer its clients unparalleled quality professional service and performance through the design and execution of its services. Successful financial execution comprises having the necessary business relationships with the appropriate domestic and international entities and counterparties for a specific transaction, and intimate familiarity with a client's investment, fund-raising and procurement goals.

The transaction negotiation is often the most important step of the engagement process. APJ Capital customises each transaction to meet shareholder and other stakeholder interests, balancing business, legal and financial tradeoffs, and concerning ourselves with every detail of the transaction.

APJ Capital is led by Albert Louw, a Certified Merger & Acquisition Advisor (CM&AA), the designation awarded by the Alliance of Merger & Acquisition Advisors in the United States. Through this affiliation, the firm has access to a broad range of transaction solutions. We assess each set of assets on its merits, recommend the most appropriate path and build a plan aligned with our client’s strategic focus.

Drawing on this experience, we understand the challenges and opportunities that drive asset performance, and we use that insight to help clients unlock value through acquisition, divestiture or combination.
