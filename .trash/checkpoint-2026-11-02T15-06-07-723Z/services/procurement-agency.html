Procurement & Agency

Commodities, agency representation and government-facing procurement across the Middle East: capital, contracts and market access.

  • Investment Banking
  • Procurement & Agency
  • Southern Africa Platform
  • Operational Corporate Execution
  • Market Inflection Point Strategy

With its diversified network of capital providers and strategic partners, APJ Capital Group has positioned itself not only as a funding and investment source, but to add value in other aspects of its clients' business.

With the tremendous need currently in the Middle East for infrastructure rebuilding and for commodities, specifically petroleum derivatives, APJ Capital has developed into a procurement and agency-representation firm in this region. Procurement in the Middle East has become an integral part of corporate performance, and we have built customer-supplier relationships in service of our clients' never-ending quest for cost savings.

Some of our tactical services include contact with influential policy makers in the Middle East, and the tracking of government contracts and procurement developments. We assist clients in raising debt and equity capital and, where appropriate, the firm also makes strategic investments in these businesses.

It is our unwavering policy to devote the utmost attention to every transaction in order to generate positive risk-adjusted returns across market cycles. We approach all transactions through rigorous fundamental analysis of the drivers of potential investment risk and return, and cost-benefit analysis, looking at both qualitative and quantitative factors before extensive due diligence.
