Market Inflection Point Strategy

Maximising returns, minimising regulatory risk and streamlining transactions at the point where a business changes shape.

  • Investment Banking
  • Procurement & Agency
  • Southern Africa Platform
  • Operational Corporate Execution
  • Market Inflection Point Strategy

The inflection point is where a proven business model meets a market that has moved. These companies need specialised, tailored financing solutions, and usually more than capital: they need a strategic partner who brings operational efficiency, market expansion and governance.

Our mandate closes transactions in a timely fashion and on terms most favourable to a client, given its financial capacity, expectations and current business environment.

Discuss a mandate

Three objectives

  • Maximise returns: increase net proceeds from a broad range of corporate transactions by drawing on financial, legal and accounting expertise and a network of partners to properly structure deals.
  • Minimise risk: reduce or mitigate the risk of legal and regulatory non-compliance by knowing the sovereign, systemic and extension risk, and other corporate hurdles the client must overcome in the Middle East.
  • Streamline transactions: reduce complexity and increase the speed of business transactions by facilitating introductions, bringing together buyers and sellers, and providing access to world-class business resources.
