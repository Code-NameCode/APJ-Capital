Who we are

Built for complex, cross-border transactions

APJ Capital Group is a USA-certified international private equity and M&A investment banking firm headquartered in San Francisco, with offices in Amman, Jordan and Johannesburg, South Africa.

Every engagement is built around in-depth due diligence, rigorous financial analysis, and tailored transaction execution — designed to serve each client's specific commercial objectives rather than a standardized template.

We maintain an unwavering focus on generating positive risk-adjusted returns across market cycles, while integrating environmental and social considerations as genuine drivers of long-term value.
