Who we are

An enterprise that operates globally, but thinks locally.

APJ Capital Group is a specialised, boutique, mid-size private equity and investment banking firm. The company advises mid-market companies on capital strategy, M&A transactions and portfolio growth, across various industries and asset classes.

The firm operates from three offices, San Francisco, Amman and Johannesburg, giving it direct reach into Western capital markets, the Middle East and Sub-Saharan Africa.

Rather than applying a templated process, the APJ team works closely with each client to design strategy, and bespoke funding arrangements and deal terms that fit their specific commercial reality and objectives. The goal is straightforward: build diversified income streams that hold up over time, not just close the next transaction.

APJ also takes a clear position on responsible investing. Environmental and social considerations are part of how the firm evaluates deals, not as an afterthought, but as a factor in long-term value creation.

01 · How we work

Market Inflection Point Strategy

Every business changes shape at some point. At the inflection point the decisions made determine whether a transaction closes well or merely closes. APJ structures that moment.

  • Close in good time, on the most favourable terms a client's financial capacity, expectations and business environment allow
  • Maximise returns: increase net proceeds across a broad range of corporate transactions, drawing on financial, legal and accounting expertise and a network of partners to structure the deal
  • Minimise risk: reduce exposure to legal and regulatory non-compliance by understanding the sovereign, systematic and other corporate hurdles a client must clear in the Middle East
  • Streamline: reduce complexity and increase speed by facilitating introductions, bringing buyers and sellers together and opening access to world-class business resources

The result is a transaction that is timely, well structured and built to hold up once it has closed.

02 · How we work

Operational Corporate Execution

Good financial execution comprises the relationships you hold, the preparation behind them, and the quality of the negotiation that closes the deal.

  • The necessary business relationships with the appropriate domestic and international entities and counterparties for a specific transaction
  • Intimate familiarity with a client's investment, fund-raising and procurement goals
  • Negotiation as the most important step of the engagement: each transaction is customised to shareholder and stakeholder interests, balancing business, legal and financial tradeoffs and attending to every detail
  • As a certified member of the M&A Alliance in the USA, access to the appropriate solution for a given set of assets, and a plan that meshes with the client's strategic focus

Capitalising on that experience, APJ understands the challenges and opportunities that drive asset performance, and how the interrelationships between those decisions affect the returns our clients get.
