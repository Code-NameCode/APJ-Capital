The firm

The APJ team.

A specialised, boutique, mid-size private equity and investment banking firm advising mid-market companies on capital strategy, M&A transactions and portfolio growth, across various industries and asset classes.

Our mission

Ideas, people and capital, brought together deliberately.

Our mission is to deliver global investment banking and international procurement solutions for growing companies globally.

We get excited about companies that create new things, develop new markets and venture where others have previously not travelled. These are the innovators, the game changers that transform markets and teach us new ways to interact with the world around us.

APJ's multifaceted international exposure is used to create distinctive value for our customers, our suppliers and our stakeholders. By tapping the successful outcome of completed projects in different industries, combined with proven financial modelling programmes, we are quickly able to ascertain a viable financial and management strategy for our clients.

How we work

Three convictions we do not negotiate.

Our operating philosophy describes high-performance, diverse teams that foster cognitive conflict and thinking to avoid complacency.

01

Collective thinking

APJ's business acumen is a philosophy that describes high-performance, diverse teams that foster cognitive conflict and thinking to avoid complacency and achieve superior results. By bringing together varied skill sets and encouraging a culture where ideas are tested rather than simply accepted, APJ avoids the pitfalls of untested storytelling.

02

Strong ethics

Fiduciary duty is an operational reality, not a marketing slogan. It is the cornerstone of APJ's client trust. Clear, upfront communication about risks, fees and conflicts of interest ensures transparency and aligns APJ's success directly with the success of our clients.

03

An eye for opportunity

We look for deals where others see complexity. The team is wired to find workable structures in markets and situations that conventional firms pass over, focusing on narrow opportunities where we have in-depth knowledge, a specialised or niche strategy built on superior information and a bottom-up view of fundamentally sound, often under-researched companies.
